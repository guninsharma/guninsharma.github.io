I finished my second year of college with a GitHub full of repos I’d never explain to anyone and a LinkedIn I opened maybe twice.

None of it added up to anything. It was just... stuff I’d done.

So I made this website.

Not because I have it all figured out - I really don’t - but because I needed somewhere to put the pieces while I figure it out. Somewhere that says: *here’s where I am right now.* Not a highlight reel. Not a five year plan. Just an honest look at someone who is still very much in it.

I’m two years into a CS and Data Science degree, interning at two places together, which sounds ambitious until you admit one of the main reasons you took both was to figure out what you actually like doing. I’m building things I don’t fully know how to build yet. I’m picking up tools I read about at 1 AM on Twitter and thought, I *need* to understand this. And then I actually go and do it, even when I have no idea where to start.

That is what actually matters to me. I don’t always have a plan. I’m not always the most qualified person for what I’m attempting. But I don’t back down just because something feels too big or too unclear. I start somewhere and figure out the rest as I go. Sometimes it works. Sometimes I end up with a repo I’d never explain to anyone.

And honestly, that’s kind of what this site is too. I’m not waiting until I have something polished to show. I’d rather show the process - the half finished ideas, the things I’m still learning, the projects that are still quite early to talk about - than wait around for a future version of myself that has it all together.

If you’re here because you’re hiring or want to collaborate - cool, reach out. I’d love that. And if you’re here because you’re also a second year student who Googled “do i need a portfolio website” at some point - hi. same.

**Gunin Sharma**
